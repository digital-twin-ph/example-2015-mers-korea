MERS-CoV, South Korea, 2015
===========================

Source:   The R package outbreaks, dataset mers_korea_2015.
          https://www.reconverse.org/outbreaks/reference/mers_korea_2015.html
          https://github.com/reconverse/outbreaks (data/mers_korea_2015.RData,
          retrieved 2026-11-11, 3,069 bytes, sha256 2a08976a01d41ca6c4e800684c3606813dcadbac6e633483ef513b3f9f182b88)

Collected: By the Epidemic Intelligence and Response section of the European
          Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (ECDC), from public
          information during the first weeks of the outbreak. Transfer to R and
          documentation by Bertrand Sudre (ECDC), as the package states.

Purpose:  The package describes the dataset as meant for teaching purposes,
          and as neither the final outbreak investigation results nor a
          complete description of the transmission chain.

Terms:    NO LICENCE FOR THE DATA IS STATED by the package or by ECDC, as far
          as was found on 2026-11-11. The package's code is MIT (GitHub) and
          GPL (>= 2) (CRAN 1.9.0); neither licence is stated to cover the data.
          The data are used here for teaching, the purpose the package states,
          by decision of the Fieldwork practitioner on 2026-11-11. That is a
          judgement about use, not a licence grant. The LICENSE file (CC0)
          covers this repository's own files -- scripts/convert.R and the
          documentation -- and NOT the data in data/.

Changed:  Nothing in the values. scripts/convert.R reads the .RData with R and
          writes its two data frames as CSV: data/mers_korea_2015_linelist.csv
          (162 cases, 15 columns) and data/mers_korea_2015_contacts.csv (98
          links, 4 columns). Missing values are written as empty cells.

Further reading on the outbreak's early stage, cited by the package:
          Penttinen PM, Kaasik-Aaslav K, Friaux A, et al. Taking stock of the
          first 133 MERS coronavirus cases globally -- Is the epidemic
          changing? Euro Surveill. 2013;18(39):pii=20596.

Hospitals: data/mers_korea_2015_facilities.csv, made for this repository on
          2026-11-12. One row for each hospital as the line list writes it in
          loc_hosp, with the facility identified, its coordinates and their
          source. Coordinates: OpenStreetMap (10 rows), (c) OpenStreetMap
          contributors, Open Database License 1.0,
          https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright; Wikidata (2 rows), CC0 1.0.
          Because it contains OpenStreetMap data, THIS FILE IS UNDER THE ODbL
          1.0: a database derived from it must be offered under the same
          licence, with this attribution. Not covered by LICENSE (CC0).
          Identification checked against the hospitals named by the Korean
          government on 7 June 2015 (as reported in Korean news, Tenasia and
          the Kyunghyang Shinmun) and by Gangdong-gu on 1 July 2015
          (https://www.gangdong.go.kr/web/newportal/press/5551). Confidence and
          the reason for it are in each row.

Provinces: The third workflow's study area is South Korea's 17 provinces from
          geoBoundaries, gbOpen ADM1, release 9469f09
          (https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries), from Natural Earth,
          representing 2021, Public Domain. Carried inside
          fieldwork-project.json.gz; not changed.
